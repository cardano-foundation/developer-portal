Beginner lectures playground

Make sure Lace is installed, switched to the Preview network, and has a little test ADA (from Lecture 1).

UTxOs & Transactions

Sends 1 ADA from your wallet back to itself. The SDK picks which UTxOs to spend, adds the change, and balances the transaction, you just sign and submit.

Time on Cardano

Sends 1 ADA to yourself, but valid only for the next ~2 minutes. It sets an upper slot bound (invalidHereafter), submit in time and it goes through, miss the window and the ledger rejects it.

Native scripts & metadata

Lock sends 5 ADA to a native-script address that only you can reclaim, and only after ~5 minutes. Unlock spends that locked UTxO back to your wallet once the 5 minutes have passed (try it too early and the ledger rejects it).

Sends a tiny transaction to yourself with a text note attached as metadata, readable later on the explorer (smart contracts can't see it).

Tokens

Mints 100 GOLD under a native-script policy that requires your signature. The new tokens land back in your wallet, right next to your ADA.
