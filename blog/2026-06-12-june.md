In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Chris Gianelloni, CEO of Blink Labs, explores Dingo, a high-performance Cardano node and block producer written entirely in Go. He discusses why implementation diversity is essential for Cardano’s long-term resilience, examines the architecture behind Dingo, shares the project's current roadmap, and explains how alternative node implementations strengthen the network while expanding the developer ecosystem.
