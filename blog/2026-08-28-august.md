In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Albina, Sandro, and Patrick from Masumi provide an update on the Masumi ecosystem, covering recent developments across the network and what’s coming next. They explore Masumi, Sokosumi, highlighted agents and the Dev Hub, alongside the business vision, collaborations, and x402. The session also covers Masumi Smart Contract V2, Hydra integration, Veridian Agent Certification, and the latest roadmap.
