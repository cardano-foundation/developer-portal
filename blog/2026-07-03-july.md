In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Thomas Kammerlocher, Senior Full-Stack Developer at the Cardano Foundation, explores the CIP-113 KYC Substandard and its implementation for programmable tokens on Cardano. He explains how the standard enables compliant asset flows through smart contract registries, off-chain identity verification, and trusted credentials, followed by a live demonstration of Veridian integration. The session also covers the current roadmap, audit status, and future wallet support for CIP-113 tokens.
