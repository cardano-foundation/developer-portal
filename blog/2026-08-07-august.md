In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Fabian Bormann, Head of Ecosystem Engineering at the Cardano Foundation, explores IBC on Cardano and the connection to Injective. He explains what the integration means in practice, how developers can start building with it, and how the architecture works under the hood. The session also covers the roadmap and what’s next for cross-chain interoperability on Cardano.
