In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Agustín Salinas, Cardano Smart Contract Developer, provides a behind-the-scenes look at Modulo-P’s journey into zero-knowledge development on Cardano. He explores the challenges, lessons learned, and key milestones along the way, highlights the ZK tools and projects built by Modulo-P, and takes a deep dive into the PTAU ceremony and its application. The session concludes with a Q&A for developers interested in zero-knowledge cryptography and Cardano infrastructure.
