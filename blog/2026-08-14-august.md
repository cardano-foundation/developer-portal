In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Ilja von Hoessle, Principal Software Engineer at PolyCrypt, explores Cardano Lightning and the foundations of state channels and the Lightning Network. He explains the architecture behind bringing Bitcoin Lightning to Cardano, shares the current state of development, and discusses the key challenges and future work required to advance the project.
