In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Oleksii Khodakivskyi, CEO of Lantr, and Alex Nemish, CTO, explore the evolution and future roadmap of the Scalus Development Platform. They reflect on the project's key milestones over the past year, share their long-term vision for improving the Cardano developer experience, outline upcoming releases and integrations, and discuss how the community can contribute to the platform’s ongoing development.
