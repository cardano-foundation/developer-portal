In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Chris Gianelloni, CEO of Blink Labs, explores the evolution of naming systems from DNS and ICANN to Handshake and cDNSd. He explains how decentralized naming works, why it matters for open and resilient internet infrastructure, and how cDNSd fits into the future of decentralized networking. The session provides insight for developers interested in decentralized infrastructure, networking, and Web3 technologies.
