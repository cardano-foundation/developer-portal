In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Dominic Wallis, Founder & Developer of Kompact.io, presents Subbit.xyz, a lightweight Layer 2 protocol enabling trustless subscription payments and micropayments on Cardano. He explores how Subbit uses payment channels and smart contracts to manage off-chain payment guarantees, giving users greater control over their funds while enabling efficient batch settlement on-chain. The session also covers the production integration with Orcfax oracle feeds and the potential for subscription-based Web3 services.
