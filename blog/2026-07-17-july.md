In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Matthieu Pizenberg explores Tessera, an on-chain survey application implementing CIP-0179. He explains how Tessera enables decentralized surveys on Cardano, explores the ideas behind CIP-0179, and shows how developers can leverage the standard to build transparent, on-chain voting and survey applications. The session provides insight into emerging Cardano standards and their potential applications in governance.
