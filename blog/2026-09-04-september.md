In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Santiago Carmuega, Founder & CEO of TxPipe, introduces Demeter.run, a cloud platform designed to simplify Cardano infrastructure and eliminate the overhead of running local nodes. He explores managed RPCs, open-source APIs, and multi-operator infrastructure, alongside tools for streamlining developer workflows and flexible hosting options. The session also covers the platform’s roadmap, including Dolos data node integration, EIP-402 agent payments, and further improvements to the developer experience.
