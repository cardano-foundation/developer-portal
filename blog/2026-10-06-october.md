In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Thomas Kammerlocher, Head of Solutions Engineering at the Cardano Foundation, explores the x402 standard and how it enables automated, decentralized payments for HTTP requests. He explains the payment workflow, from client requests and server offers to payment and resource delivery, and how it enables pay-per-use services for API data and AI agent computations. The session also covers how developers can start building with x402 today, with the Masumi team joining the discussion.
