In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Romain Soulat and Jean-Frédéric Etienne from Input Output Group’s High Assurance team present Blaster, an open-source formal verification engine for Cardano smart contracts. They explain how Blaster uses Lean 4 and UPLC to automatically verify contracts against specifications or produce concrete counterexamples when they fail. The session includes a practical walkthrough of writing specifications, a live demonstration of successful proofs and counterexamples, and an overview of the roadmap and supported development frameworks, including Plinth, Aiken, and Plutarch.
