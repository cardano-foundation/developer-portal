In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, Giovanni Gargiulo, Senior Blockchain Architect at the Cardano Foundation, introduces uplc.link, a platform for trustless smart contract verification on Cardano. He explains how the platform independently compiles and verifies source code against deployed on-chain UPLC bytecode, ensuring reproducible builds and verifiable script hashes. The session also covers practical workflows for developers and auditors to inspect and share verified contracts, and how this approach strengthens transparency and trust across the Cardano ecosystem.
