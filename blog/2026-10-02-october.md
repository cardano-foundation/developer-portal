In this session of the Cardano Developer Office Hours, the Hydra team leads a technical workshop exploring Hydra, Cardano’s Layer 2 scaling solution based on off-chain state channels. They explain how transactions are processed off-chain and settled on the main chain, what this enables in terms of sub-second finality, zero fees, and high throughput, and explore practical use cases and technical setup. The session concludes with a live Q&A with the Hydra team.
