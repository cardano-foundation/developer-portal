# What to build

Cardano is a platform for building things people can **own, trust, and exchange** directly, without a bank, a platform, or a middleman sitting in between, taking a cut, and holding all the keys. You don't need to be a crypto expert to build something useful on it.

Here are real things teams are building, in plain terms, with a link to how each one works.

## Money and assets

### Payments and stablecoins

Accept and send money anywhere in seconds, with fees measured in cents. Great for payroll, remittances, in-app purchases, or issuing your own stablecoin, with no card networks or banking rails to wire up.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/dapps/overview)

### Tokenized real-world assets

Turn property, invoices, funds, or commodities into digital shares people can buy, sell, or own a fraction of, with the ownership rules built in and enforced automatically.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/native-tokens/programmable-tokens)

## Digital ownership

### Collectibles, art, and memberships

Sell art, music, digital goods, or membership passes that buyers genuinely own and can resell, with a royalty routed back to the creator on every resale.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/native-tokens/mint-nft)

### Ticketing

Issue event tickets that can't be forged or copied, and set your own resale rules to curb scalping and protect your fans.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/native-tokens/mint-nft)

## Trust and provenance

### Supply chain and provenance

Track a product from its origin to the shelf and let anyone verify it's genuine, useful for food, luxury goods, pharmaceuticals, or anything where authenticity matters.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/native-tokens/authenticated-products)

### Identity and credentials

Let people prove who they are, or what they've earned, a diploma, a license, a certification, without handing over more personal data than necessary.

[Learn how](https://www.hyperledger.org/projects/identus)

## Community and finance

### DeFi: swaps, lending, and savings

Build financial apps where the rules are enforced by code instead of a company: trade one token for another, lend and earn, or run a marketplace, all with no middleman holding the funds.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/dapps/defi), or build a swap step by step in the [Tutorial](/docs/developers/onboarding/tutorial/overview).

### Community treasuries and governance

Let a community pool its funds and decide together, out in the open, how to spend them, the backbone of DAOs and grassroots organizations.

[Learn how](/docs/developers/curriculum/staking-governance/governance)
