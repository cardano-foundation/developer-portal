This is the hands-on on-ramp. By the end you will have a working environment, your tool of choice installed, and a real transaction submitted to a Cardano testnet, all without spending real ADA. It assumes you have the mental model from [Cardano Fundamentals](/docs/foundations/overview) and its [Core Concepts](/docs/value/overview); if a concept here is unfamiliar, those pages explain it.

## The path

1. **[Networks & test ADA](/docs/first-steps/networks-and-test-ada)**: pick a network, get free test ADA from the faucet, and find an explorer
2. **[Choose your tools](/docs/first-steps/choose-your-tools)**: pick an SDK for your language and get a provider key
3. **[Your first transaction](/docs/first-steps/your-first-transaction)**: build, sign, and submit a payment, then read it back from the chain
