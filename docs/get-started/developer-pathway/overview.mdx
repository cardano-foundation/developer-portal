## From zero to core contributor or DApp builder

The diagram below is one visual overview of typical journeys on Cardano: where you start, what to learn, the tracks you can pick (contracts, frontend, infrastructure), how it all integrates, and where deeper specialisation leads. It is **illustrative only**: many tools, courses, and links are intentionally left out so the chart stays readable.

## How to read this diagram

The chart organises the journey using four ideas. They are not strict job titles or fixed steps, just a way to keep the picture readable.

- **Phase**: a broad stage of your journey (orientation, cross-cutting knowledge, intermediate projects, and shipping). Most builders move through every phase, in roughly this order.
- **Track**: the main area you specialise in *while* moving through the phases (smart contracts, frontend, or infrastructure). You pick one as your primary focus, but you will touch the others.
- **Ramp**: a short series of progressively harder *practice projects* used to bridge fundamentals and production work (vending machine, NFT minter, simple DEX, full-stack app).
- **Specialisation**: deeper focus areas you can pursue *after* shipping a first mainnet project (DeFi, layer 2, NFT and gaming, governance, bridges, or core protocol).
